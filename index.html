About STEM Bridge AI

Free, friendly feedback on STEM work

STEM Bridge AI gives students quick, personalized feedback on science writing and code, and helps teachers check handwritten work and paper tests. It is free and open source, and it runs in any web browser with nothing to install.

  • Science Writing (SciWrite AI): feedback on lab reports and explanations, for Kindergarten through college.
  • Coding Helper (CodeBridge AI): hints that lead students to fix their own code, from grade 6 up.
  • Photo Check: read handwritten work from a photo, or mark a paper test against an answer key.

Why we built it: many students in rural and low-income schools have little access to expert STEM feedback, and 48 states reported teacher shortages in 2023–2024. This tool aims to give every student quick, personalized guidance.
