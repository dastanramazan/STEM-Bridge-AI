Mission: STEM Bridge AI addresses two critical gaps in U.S. education — the shortage of qualified teachers reported in 48 states and the absence of expert STEM writing instruction in rural and low-income schools. By providing instant, expert-level feedback powered by large language models, this platform gives every student access to the individualized guidance previously available only to the privileged few.
